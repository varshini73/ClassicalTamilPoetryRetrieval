## Methodology
The PM296 poem-level Tamil colophon was re-audited for explicit controlled Uri wording only. Directly named separation, union, or quarrel terms receive a draft Uri. Indirect situations, conflicting signals, and absent direct terms remain needs_review. No Tinai-to-Uri mapping, translations, or inferred modern labels were used.
