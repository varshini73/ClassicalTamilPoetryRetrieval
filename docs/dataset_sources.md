## Excluded for now

Kuruntokai, Ainkurunuru, Akananuru, and Kalithogai have candidate Project Madurai editions but are excluded because their extraction and poem-level metadata have not received the same record-by-record quality check. No missing records are fabricated.

## Known limitations

The PM296 introductory matter reports textual incompleteness/uncertainty. The machine-readable source lacks usable verse records for 125, 201, and 234, so no rows were created. Record 385 is preserved only as supplied. All records require human literary review.
